🧭 TripQuest Rules

1. Itinerary Planning: Import lists or define them. We group them to minimize travel time.

2. Real-Time Sync: Open TripQuest in another tab or browser window, join the same Room Code, and experience instant updates!

3. Travel Bingo: Everyone gets a bingo card. Check off items during the trip to score XP for your team.

4. Spin Wheel: Draw challenges and spin the wheel to randomly assign them to players. Complete them to score big points!

5. Secret Missions: Assigned privately. Complete them, then hit Claim to surprise your friends!
